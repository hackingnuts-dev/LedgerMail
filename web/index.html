Privacy

Gmail access tokens stay only in memory. Financial email summaries and review decisions are saved on this device in IndexedDB, separately by Gmail account. They are not cloud-backed and could be deleted if Safari data is cleared. Use a device passcode; avoid shared devices.

Exports contain private financial details. Protect your backup files. No Google passwords or access tokens are included.
