Privacy

LedgerMail reads full email text temporarily on your device; it stores only short evidence excerpts and review decisions. It does not save full email bodies or Gmail access tokens. Local history is separate by account, can be cleared by Safari, and is not cloud-backed. Lock your device.

Exports contain private financial details. Protect your backup files. No Google passwords or access tokens are included.
